Anda - Algeria Travel Guide

Project information

  • Category: Mobile App (Android & iOS)
  • Client: Personal Project
  • Project date: 2024
  • Tech Stack: React Native, Laravel API, Maps
  • Project URL: Coming soon

Anda is a TripAdvisor-like mobile app dedicated to tourism in Algeria. It helps tourists and locals discover the best places, restaurants, hotels and cultural sights across all wilayas, with reviews, ratings, photos and maps.

  • Explore destinations by wilaya / category
  • Places, restaurants & hotels listings with photos
  • Reviews & ratings from the community
  • Maps integration & favorites
  • Multilingual: Arabic, French, English
